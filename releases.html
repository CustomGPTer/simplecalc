Install a new version over the old one. Your history and settings are kept.

1.0.0 22 November 2026

The first public version.

  • Calculator with exact decimal maths, so 0.1 + 0.2 is 0.3, and the answer shown as you type.
  • Keys the same size as a standard phone calculator, with a scientific pad that slides in: sin, cos, tan and their inverses, logs, roots, powers, factorials, π, e and φ.
  • Memory keys, Ans, a paper roll of every answer in the session, and history with search, pins and labels.
  • Smart entry: type questions such as “20% off £85”, “£120 + VAT” or “days until Christmas” and get the answer with the working.
  • Fourteen tools: VAT, percentage, discount, tip and split, best value, fuel cost, loans, currency, a unit converter, dates, age, hours worked, BMI and grades.
  • A unit converter with 13 groups that shows every unit at once.
  • A working calculator widget for your home screen and a quick-settings tile.
  • Light, dark and black themes with six colours, and smooth animations you can switch off.
  • History export as a spreadsheet file, and a backup file you keep.
  • No internet permission, no account, no adverts.
