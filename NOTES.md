The first public version of SimpleCalc, a free and private calculator with everyday tools.

- Calculator with exact decimal maths, so 0.1 + 0.2 is 0.3, and the answer shown as you type.
- Keys the same size as a standard phone calculator, with a scientific pad that slides in.
- Memory keys, Ans, a paper roll, and history with search, pins and labels.
- Smart entry: type questions like "20% off £85", "£120 + VAT" or "days until Christmas".
- Fourteen tools: VAT, percentage, discount, tip and split, best value, fuel cost, loans, currency, units, dates, age, hours worked, BMI and grades.
- A working calculator widget for your home screen and a quick-settings tile.
- Light, dark and black themes with six colours.
- History export as a spreadsheet file, and a backup file you keep.
- No internet permission, no account, no adverts.

Android 8 or later. Install over any earlier copy; your history and settings are kept.
